
# Molecular Dynamics Trajectory Description

All molecular dynamics simulations reported in this repository were performed using Desmond v7.0 (Schrödinger Suite 2025-1).

Simulation systems:

* hERG–Moxifloxacin complex (PDB ID: 5VA1, Chain B)
* hERG–FLQ_Mod_2 complex (PDB ID: 5VA1, Chain B)
* DNA gyrase–FLQ_Mod_2 complex (PDB ID: 2XCT)

Simulation parameters:

* Force field: OPLS4
* Water model: TIP4P-D
* Ensemble: NPT
* Temperature: 300 K
* Pressure: 1 atm
* Salt concentration: 0.15 M NaCl
* Production simulation time: 200 ns
* Replicates: 3 independent simulations

Representative structural frames shown in this repository were extracted from equilibrated regions within the final 100 ns analysis window used for MM/PBSA free-energy calculations and interaction occupancy analysis.

All representative structures correspond to the top-ranked Glide XP/GNINA docking pose used for molecular dynamics initialization.

Additional replicate trajectories and raw simulation outputs are available from the corresponding author upon reasonable request.
