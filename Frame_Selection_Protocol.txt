# Representative Frame Selection Protocol

Representative molecular dynamics frames were selected from the equilibrated segment of each 200 ns production trajectory.

Selection criteria included:

* stable backbone RMSD
* preserved ligand orientation
* conserved key interaction geometry
* absence of transient unstable conformations
* consistency with MM/PBSA energetic trends reported in the manuscript

Frames were selected from the final 100 ns trajectory interval following equilibration.

For hERG complexes, representative frames preserved:

* Tyr545 aromatic interactions
* Arg541 interaction geometry
* Glu544 hydrogen bonding patterns
* experimentally plausible pore orientation

For DNA gyrase complexes, representative frames preserved:

* Mg²⁺ chelation geometry
* Ser84 and Asp87 interaction network
* compact quinolone pharmacophore alignment

All residue numbering, protonation states, ligand naming conventions, and chain identifiers correspond exactly to the systems described in the manuscript.
