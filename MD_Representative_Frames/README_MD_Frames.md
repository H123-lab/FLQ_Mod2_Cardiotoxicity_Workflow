README_MD_Frames.md
Representative equilibrated molecular dynamics snapshots generated from 200 ns Desmond simulations using the OPLS4 force field and TIP4P-D solvent model.

Snapshots were selected from the equilibrated final 100 ns production window across three independent replicates.

These images are provided for structural reproducibility and qualitative interaction visualization corresponding to analyses reported in the manuscript and supplementary information.
